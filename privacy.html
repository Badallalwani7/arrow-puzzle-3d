Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Snarl is a single-player puzzle game made by Scrolltrace. It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location.

What the game collects

Nothing. Your progress (the level you have reached and a level you left half-finished) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app. The game does not send any data to us or to anyone else, and it contains no analytics or advertising code.

The website's beta sign-up

If you join the beta through this website, we keep the first name and email address you enter, the time, and which game's page the form was on. They are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the developer and used only to run the beta and to tell you about the launch. They are not shared or sold. To have your entry deleted, write to the developer contact address shown on Snarl's Google Play page.

Future ads

A later version may offer an optional video ad to skip a level. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing the ad provider and what it collects.

Children

Snarl is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact

Use the developer contact address shown on Snarl's Google Play page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
