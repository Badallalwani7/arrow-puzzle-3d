How it plays

Tap. Turn. Untangle.

Every arrow you free opens a way out for another. Simple to learn, hard to put down.

01Tap an arrowIt slides out the way it points. Only arrows with a clear path escape.
02Turn the shapeDrag to look around cubes, stairs, towers and rings. The next free arrow may be round the back.
03Mind your heartsHit another arrow and you lose a heart. Three misses and the level starts again.

Calm to look at. Tricky to solve.

Questions

Is 3D Arrow Puzzle free?

Yes. 3D Arrow Puzzle: Arrow Games is free to download and play on Android.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole game runs on your phone, with no account and no internet needed.

How many levels are there?

Levels never run out. They come in easy, medium and hard, with a tough one every few levels, and every player gets the same puzzle at the same level.

What if I get stuck?

Each level gives you three hints that light up a free arrow, and you can skip a level from the settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It is on Android first. Try it in your browser in the meantime.
